Questions

How do I flip?

Hold the pan and flick your finger up. Faster flicks go higher; leaning the flick toward the handle adds spin. Move the pan under the pancake to catch it, and lower it as it lands for a soft catch.

How do I fold the French omelet?

Pull the pan sharply toward the handle. A small, quick pull folds it; too hard and it flips over whole.

There's no sound or vibration.

Check the mute switch and Settings in the game, where sound, music and haptics each have their own control.

How do I start over?

Settings, then Reset progress. This can't be undone.

Is the food-safety information reliable?

Safe temperatures and storage advice come from the US FDA and USDA, and each cook-along lists its sources. Kitchen Quest teaches technique through play; it doesn't certify you or replace care in a real kitchen. Appearance alone can't confirm food is safe, so use a thermometer where temperatures are listed.
