Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026.

Kitchen Quest does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, settings, nickname, chef avatar, cooking passport entries and any photos you add to the passport are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them, and you can erase your progress at any time in Settings.

Sharing

When you share a scorecard, the image and challenge link go through your device's share sheet to the app you choose. We don't receive a copy. A challenge link contains the recipe, a game seed, your score and your nickname, so anyone you send it to can see those.

Leaderboards

This version shows leaderboards stored on your device only. If online leaderboards are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before any score leaves your device, and it will say exactly what is sent.

Children

Kitchen Quest collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue.
